03 · Mark
Frame. The Dx hexagon, 96 grid, stroke 4.5, in the hue. Same as every sibling.
Prompt. A chevron, stroke 4, in the soft hue. It is the input caret the terminal draws.
Cursor. An underscore bar on the chevron's baseline. The glyph is centered on 48,48.
Clear space. Half the mark height on every side. Lockup mark height is 1.3× the wordmark cap height.
Do not rotate the hexagon, add glow, fill the frame on dark, or recolor the glyph with another product's hue.
